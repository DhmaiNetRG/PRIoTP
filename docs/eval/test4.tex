\chapter{Test 4: Reliability under heterogeneous delays}
In this section we study the effect of different delays in case of
weakly reliable and non-realiable transport.

\section{Evaluation scenario}
The delays were created dummynet, and these delays affect all communication
between client and server. The delays are for four clients 0ms, 25ms, 100ms, and
400ms.
